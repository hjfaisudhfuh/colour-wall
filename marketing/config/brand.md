# Brand — The Colour Wall

## Product

- **Name:** The Colour Wall
- **Live URL:** https://colour-wall.vercel.app
- **Current wall:** The First Wall
- **Format:** 100×100 public canvas (10,000 squares)
- **Price:** $1 per square
- **Mechanic:** pick a colour, leave a mark on the wall

## Voice — four adjectives

1. **Simple** — short sentences, plain words, no jargon.
2. **Playful** — internet-native fun. The product is a $1-per-square wall, not a SaaS.
3. **Transparent** — build-in-public energy. Say what's true, including what's small or unfinished.
4. **Internet-native** — speaks the language of online communities. Lowercase-friendly captions. Comfortable with memes, but never forced.

## Tone references

- **Million Dollar Homepage** — simple, weird, internet-history energy.
- **Product Hunt / Indie Hackers** — build-in-public clarity. Founders talking like humans about what they're shipping.

## Avoid

- Corporate SaaS tone ("unlock", "leverage", "synergy", "best-in-class", "revolutionary", "disrupt", "world-class", "next-generation").
- Fake hype ("blowing up", "everyone is talking about", "huge launch").
- Crypto / NFT / Web3 / token / mint / drop / blockchain language.
- "Get rich", "make money", "investment", "ROI" framing.
- Overpolished startup jargon. Don't write like a Series-A pitch deck.
- Pressure language: "limited time", "selling out", "only X left", "last chance".

## House style — concrete

- Use sentence case in titles when possible.
- Avoid em-dash overuse in body copy.
- Numbers: spell out one through nine, digits for 10 and above (except in product facts where digits are part of the brand, e.g. "$1", "100×100", "10,000").
- "The Colour Wall" (capitalised). "The First Wall" (capitalised). British spelling: **colour**, not color.
- Refer to a paid space as a **square** (not "pixel", not "tile", not "block"). "Pixel art" is fine when describing the visual style.
- CTA language is invitational, not coercive: "leave a mark", "pick a square", "claim a colour" — never "buy now" / "claim before it's gone".

## What the brand IS

- A small, playful internet experiment.
- A public canvas anyone can leave a mark on for $1.
- A live thing being built in the open.

## What the brand IS NOT

- A speculative asset.
- An NFT or token.
- A "limited-edition drop".
- A SaaS platform.
- A community membership.
