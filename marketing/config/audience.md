# Audience

## Personas (high-level)

### P1 — The curious internet wanderer
- 18–35, online a lot, into "weird internet" / web history.
- Reference points: Million Dollar Homepage, /r/place, niche internet experiments.
- Motivation: be part of something small and odd. Spend $1 to leave a mark.

### P2 — The builder watcher
- 20–40, follows indie hackers / build-in-public / Product Hunt / Show HN.
- Motivation: enjoys watching a project go from 0 to live. Might buy a square to support, more than to "own".

### P3 — The pixel-art curious
- Any age, into pixel art, small creative outlets, collaborative canvases.
- Motivation: a colour and a place on a public wall. Low-stakes creative play.

### P4 — Friends & immediate network
- People who know Austin personally or follow his existing accounts.
- Motivation: support + curiosity. Should NOT be over-targeted or made to feel pressured.

## main_account_niche

**Main account niche:**
Austin's existing TikTok account is a lyric-video account. The normal content is music/lyrics-focused: short lyric videos, song clips, text-on-screen lyrics, emotional/music-led edits, and visual posts built around tracks.

**Rules for The Colour Wall mentions on the main TikTok:**
- The Colour Wall must never become the main account's niche.
- The Colour Wall should only appear as an occasional soft side-project mention.
- Max 1 soft mention per week.
- The hook must still be music/lyrics-related, not startup/project-related.
- The Colour Wall mention must be incidental: background B-roll, tiny caption line, pinned comment, or quick outro.
- No direct "buy a square" or "claim now" CTA on the main lyric account.
- No founder/startup/build-in-public content on the main lyric account.
- No repeated posts about the website.
- Best fit: lyric-video process content where The Colour Wall appears subtly as something I built, not the reason for the post.

**Acceptable soft mentions:**
- "Made this lyric edit while checking a side project I launched."
- A short screen-recording flash of The Colour Wall at the end with no sales CTA.
- A pinned comment saying "side project is linked in bio" only if the video itself stays lyric-focused.

**Banned main-account content:**
- "I built a website where you can buy a square."
- "Claim your square on The Colour Wall."
- "Day 1 of trying to fill 10,000 squares."
- Any TikTok where The Colour Wall is the hook, plot, or CTA.

## colourwall_account_niche

Separate dedicated TikTok account. Themes:

- **Internet experiment** — framing The Colour Wall as a small live experiment.
- **The public wall** — the canvas itself: zoom-ins, fill progress, time-lapse-style updates.
- **The $1 square challenge** — the mechanic, the price, the simplicity.
- **Build in public** — what's shipping this week, what broke, what was learned.
- **Pixel art / digital mark-making** — the creative side: colour choices, patterns, small designs.

Content style on this account:
- Internet-native, lowercase-friendly captions.
- No fake scarcity, no invented numbers, no "going viral" claims.
- Mix of: showing the wall, showing the build, showing a square being chosen.

## What we do NOT target

- Crypto / NFT / Web3 communities.
- "Investment opportunity" framing or speculative audiences.
- Anyone we'd have to mislead about scarcity, returns, or virality to convert.
